# Freedom Book Club — Charter

*A sub-community. Runs on whatever substrate is available.*

---

## What this is

A community committed to understanding problems that cannot be made safe by containing them. Some problems are systemic — they move through the whole host, and quarantine is just another word for delay. This book club exists to develop the kind of sight that can hold that reality without flinching, and the kind of thinking that can work inside it.

We read to understand the architecture of unfreedom well enough to build something different.

---

## Why we start with books

Kaepernick gave up control of things that were never really his. Someone who skips a meal for a stranger gives up more. The culture has the measurement backwards — it celebrates visible sacrifice and ignores the weight carried quietly by people with less to lose and more actually at stake.

We have heard more DJT speeches than MLK ones. We mean to change that.

---

## How it works

Someone brings a book. We read it together, or around it — through interviews, reviews, the conversations the book generated. We meet here. The exchanges become markdown files. The thinking accumulates.

No one needs to ask permission to contribute. That's the whole point.

---

## What we believe

- Some problems can't be quarantined and still be solved
- Killing the host is not an acceptable answer
- Incentives always win — so change the incentives
- The shortest path to a problem is often through the community already connected to it
- Naming the mechanism clearly is its own form of contribution
- You don't have to have the plan to do the most important thing

---

**The Perilous Fight** — Colin Kaepernick (ed.)
